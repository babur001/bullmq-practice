Consuming · groups & rebalancing

One log. Who reads which partition — and what happens when readers come and go?

A consumer group is a queue within itself (partitions split across members, each record handled once) and a topic across groups (every group gets its own full copy). Add consumers and watch the split; add a group and watch it fork; crash a consumer and catch the duplicate its replacement reprocesses.

last event idle — 1 consumer owns all 3 partitions
in-flight — read, not yet durable (the risk zone) committed — durable, safe DUP / LOST — what a crash does to the risk zone
Read each tape top→bottom; offsets only climb. The —— committed —— line is group chat's durable bookmark (stored in __consumer_offsets): everything below it survives a restart. Records above it are in-flight — processed by the current owner but not yet committed. Crash the owner and the new owner resumes from the committed line: under at-least-once it reprocesses the in-flight tail → DUP; flip to at-most-once (commit-first) and those records were committed but never applied → LOST. Add a 4th consumer to a 3-partition topic and it goes idle — partitions, not messages, are the unit, so 3 is your ceiling. Turn on group analytics to watch a second group hold its own offset on the very same log.
