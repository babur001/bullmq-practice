Three things to make happen here:
  1. Append u1 four times, roll, then run the cleaner. Offsets 0–2 vanish; the log now starts at 3. Nothing was renumbered — an offset is a permanent address.
  2. Now append u1 twice more without rolling, and clean again. u1 still appears twice. The active segment is never cleaned — which is why every consumer must apply last-write-wins anyway. Compaction is not deduplication.
  3. Tombstone u2, roll, clean — the tombstone survives. Now advance the clock past delete.retention.ms and clean again: it disappears, and the panel warns you. Any consumer offline during that window never learns u2 was deleted, and keeps a ghost forever.
Switch the policy to delete and the cleaner stops caring about keys entirely — it drops whole segments by age. Same log, completely different question being asked of it.
