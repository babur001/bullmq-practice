**Lesson 05 exercise submitted & PASSED all 5 steps (2026-07-30).** User worked on topic `chat.profiles-new` (3 partitions; `chat.profiles` was a stale first attempt left at default `delete` policy with 10 records — cause: **KafkaJS `createTopics()` silently no-ops on an existing topic and does NOT apply `configEntries`**; the fix is `alterConfigs`). They made two discoveries that were NOT in my lesson and I folded both in: (a) **`segment.ms` is not a timer — rolling is checked lazily in the append path**, so an idle partition never rolls and is therefore never cleaned (proved by their partition 1 sitting hours past a 100 ms `segment.ms` with one segment and no cleaner-checkpoint entry); (b) **`file.delete.delay.ms`=60000** two-stage `.deleted` rename-then-unlink. Also added to §1.4: **tombstone removal takes TWO cleaning passes** (pass 1 stamps `deleteHorizonMs = now + delete.retention.ms`, pass 2 drops it) and pass 2 must be *triggered* by new dirty data — so a tombstone long past its horizon persists on a quiet partition. Corrections given: an append never compacts anything, it only may trigger a roll (append → roll → closes segment → only then cleanable); and the cleaner's key→offset map stops at the active segment, which is why a key superseded only in the active segment keeps its older copy. Their step-5 answer was right; sharpened the asymmetry — **compact = complete key coverage, zero history; delete = complete history, incomplete key coverage** (retention drops the oldest records, and the oldest record of a quiet key holds its state, so you can't reliably fold a `delete` topic down to current state).
